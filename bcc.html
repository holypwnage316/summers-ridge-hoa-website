Building Control Committee

BCC Requests

Submit exterior change requests through a simple review process: provide the project details, the committee reviews the request, and you receive a written response.

Process

How BCC requests work

Step 1

Submit details

Use the Google Form to describe the request and include supporting sketches, photos, or links when helpful.

Step 2

Committee review

The BCC reviews the request against the HOA documents and may follow up if more information is needed.

Step 3

Written response

You receive written approval or suggestions, typically within the 30-day review window described in the Declaration.

When to Submit

Exterior changes need BCC review.

Anything that changes the outside appearance of your home or yard should be reviewed before work begins.

🏡 Common requests Fences, sheds, paint colors, landscaping structures, visible exterior updates, and similar changes
📝 Helpful details A short description, sketch or photo, location on the lot, materials, colors, and contractor timeline if available
⏱️ Review window The committee reviews plans and provides written approval or suggestions within 30 days

Contact

Questions before you submit?

Email the Building Control Committee and include any photos, sketches, or details you already have.
